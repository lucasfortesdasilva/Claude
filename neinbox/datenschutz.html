2. Der Grundsatz dieser Seite

Diese Seite ist so gebaut, dass möglichst wenig Daten überhaupt entstehen. Es werden keine Cookies gesetzt, es gibt keine Nutzerkonten, kein Tracking über Seiten hinweg und keine Weitergabe an Werbenetzwerke. Der wichtigste Punkt betrifft Ihren Lebenslauf — siehe Abschnitt 4.

4. Ihr Lebenslauf wird nicht übertragen

Wenn Sie die Lebenslauf-Funktion nutzen, wird Ihre Datei nicht hochgeladen. Sie wird ausschließlich in Ihrem Browser gelesen und ausgewertet. Es gibt keinen Server, der sie entgegennehmen könnte, keine Datenbank und kein Logfile mit Ihren Inhalten. Der ausgelesene Text liegt allein im Arbeitsspeicher Ihres Browsers und wird spätestens beim Schließen des Tabs verworfen. Die Schaltfläche „Unterlagen vernichten“ überschreibt ihn sofort.

Es findet insoweit keine Verarbeitung personenbezogener Daten durch uns statt. Sie können das selbst überprüfen: Im Netzwerk-Tab Ihres Browsers taucht Ihre Datei in keiner einzigen Anfrage auf.

Privacy policy

Convenience translation. The German version above is the legally binding one.

The principle

This site is built so that as little data as possible comes into existence. No cookies, no accounts, no cross-site tracking, nothing passed to advertising networks.

Your CV is never transmitted

If you use the CV feature, your file is not uploaded. It is read and analysed entirely inside your browser. There is no server that could receive it, no database and no log file containing its contents. The extracted text exists only in your browser's memory and is discarded at the latest when you close the tab; the “shred documents” button overwrites it immediately. You can verify this yourself: your file appears in none of the requests in your browser's network tab.

Site delivery

The site is hosted by [HOST — currently GitHub Pages, GitHub Inc., USA]. Serving a page necessarily transmits your IP address, timestamp, requested file, browser type and possibly a referrer to the host. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. We have no access to these logs. As the host is a US company, a transfer to the USA takes place on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision.

Fonts and libraries

All fonts and the pdf.js and JSZip libraries are served from this site itself. Nothing is fetched from Google Fonts or from a third-party CDN, so no IP address is transmitted to anyone for those purposes.

Local storage

No cookies are set. Your language choice and the simulator's counters are kept in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and we cannot see them.

Analytics not yet active

Visitor numbers are measured without cookies and without cross-device recognition. No personal profiles are built. Only aggregate figures and the names of interface steps are recorded — never their contents. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, no measurement takes place at all.

Experience reports not yet active

If you voluntarily send us a report we process what you entered. An email address is collected only if you tick one of the relevant boxes. The legal basis is solely your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR), and each box is a separate consent: anonymous publication, contact about this report, and occasional updates. Without a tick, none of it happens. You may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future by emailing the address in the legal notice.

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent — Art. 15 to 21 and Art. 7 (3) GDPR. You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority in the member state where you live.
