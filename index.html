Retail ReCycle

“Global brands and retailers are burning billions every year in unused retail fixtures. I want to change that.”

The Problem

Retail stores refit every 3–7 years. At every cycle, fixtures, displays, POS, and POP materials are discarded — incinerated or sent to landfill. There is no organised system to rehome, donate, or recycle this material. The waste is structural, invisible, and enormous.

The Vision

Retail ReCycle is a proposed circular economy platform connecting brands, retailers, and independent shop owners. Brands demonstrate their sustainability commitment. Retailers donate what they no longer need. Shop owners access quality fixtures at low or no cost. Everyone benefits. Nothing burns.

About

I'm Lucas — a retail marketing consultant, sustainability advocate, and founder of et tu tableau. Over 25 years I've worked at the intersection of design, brand, and execution, building retail environments and brand concepts for some of the world's most recognised names in sports, lifestyle, and consumer electronics.

My career started in a Dallas fashion showroom. It took me through eight years in the US Marine Corps Reserves — including active duty during operations in Iraq and Afghanistan — then to adidas in Nuremberg, VF Corporation in Lugano, and eventually Berlin, where I've been based for over a decade.

I believe retail can be done better. More intentional. More sustainable. More human.

“Better is not perfect, and I'm not the best — but looking forward I see deeper bonds based on shared values. Closer connections to the people and organisations I work with and for. Open communication and fearless exploration of what and how things can be improved, together.”

Based in Berlin. Currently open to consulting engagements and creative partnerships.
